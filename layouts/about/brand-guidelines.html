01 · Logo

One wordmark, two color variants — picked automatically by the site's light/dark theme toggle. Use the matching variant for the background it sits on; don't recolor or rebuild it by hand.

For dark backgrounds

For light backgrounds

Don't

Stretch, recolor, or add effects
