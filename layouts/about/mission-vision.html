{{/* ── Mission / Vision ── */}}
Mission

Our mission is to strengthen the MySQL-compatible ecosystem as neutral infrastructure for trust — an open, collaborative home where users, vendors, and the communities and projects around them can connect, contribute, gain visibility, and shape MySQL's future together, free from any single company's commercial interest.

Vision

A MySQL-compatible ecosystem with an independent voice of its own — where the community, not any single company, drives the tools, standards, and pipeline of new developers that keep MySQL competitive for decades to come.

{{/* ── Values ── */}}
Values

Neutral by Design

"We don't belong to any vendor."

Decisions are made for the benefit of the ecosystem, not any single company's roadmap. It's the only reason vendors can be visible here without it reading as a sales pitch, and the only reason users can share real-world experience without expecting a follow-up call.

Merit-Based Governance

"Participation is evaluated on merit — open to companies and individuals alike."

The Foundation follows a vendor-neutral governance model where getting involved and shaping the ecosystem isn't gated by size or seniority. It's a door, not a velvet rope.

Open by Default

"The ecosystem works better when knowledge is shared."

We support open, searchable, and accessible information across the MySQL-compatible ecosystem, helping community knowledge, resources, projects, events, and participation become easier to discover and build on.

Built for MySQL's Next Chapter

"We exist so MySQL has a future, not just a legacy."

Training, certification, and AI-era initiatives exist to keep the next generation of developers choosing MySQL — not just maintaining it.

FAQ
What does The OurSQL Foundation support?

The OurSQL Foundation supports users, customers, partners, and providers as they collaborate around MySQL as a database technology, as well as their own goals and objectives — bringing companies and organizations together in one place to give the community a future voice on the issues and opportunities it cares about.

Why is The OurSQL Foundation necessary?

It's a 501(c)(6) non-profit created to represent the MySQL community and provide a voice for everyone using, developing, or building products and services around it. With millions of installations worldwide, the Foundation acts as an independent home to host content, support community development, and grow involvement — independent of any one vendor, so it can support the whole community rather than any single product or strategy.

Who is behind the OurSQL Foundation?

It was created by a group of individuals from across the MySQL ecosystem and is governed by a board representing companies in the ecosystem and some of the largest MySQL deployments globally — not led by any single vendor. Oracle is not a member; the Foundation is supportive of Oracle's goals around MySQL while remaining independent, so it can take a long-term view regardless of changes in the wider market.

How does The OurSQL Foundation compare to the MariaDB Foundation?

The OurSQL Foundation is fully independent of any specific technology vendor, while the MariaDB Foundation is focused on MariaDB specifically. Any company in the MySQL ecosystem can get involved, so MySQL and compatible products compete on their merits — keeping user choice and user needs at the center, rather than any one vendor.

What is the future for MySQL?

We believe MySQL has a valid long-term future as long as it keeps providing value to the people who use it — value that hasn't always been voiced outside the close-knit open source community. The Foundation gives users, customers, partners, and vendors a shared place to surface where MySQL fits today, and to respond as new needs and developments arise.

How can I get involved?

Membership and contribution are open to individuals and companies alike, evaluated on merit rather than size. Start on the Get Involved or Join / Membership page to find the track that fits — contributing code and content, joining a committee, or becoming a member organization.
