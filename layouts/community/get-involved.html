Get involved

The MySQL ecosystem runs on people who show up — answering a question, filing a good bug report, writing a blog post, reviewing a doc. There's a place for you here, whatever time you have to give.

See how to start
{{/* ── Individuals note ── */}}

This page is for individuals — DBAs, developers, consultants, writers, speakers, students, or simply long-time MySQL users — who want to contribute personally, separately from any company affiliation. Looking to get your organization involved instead? See Join / Membership.

{{/* ── The path, step by step ── */}}
The path, step by step
  1. 1

    Show up where the conversation is

    {{ with $slack }}Join us on Slack{{ else }}Join us on Slack{{ end }} and introduce yourself. No commitment required yet, just be present.

  2. 2

    Make your first contribution

    Pick whatever matches your time and interests:

    • Answer a question from another community member
    • Share a blog post, talk, or tutorial about MySQL or the ecosystem
    • Browse the Resource Hub and help us keep it current — software, services, training, events

  3. 3

    Take on a bigger role

    Join one of the Foundation's Committee Groups — starting with the Security Committee, the first of several Committees the Foundation will stand up over time — or move toward a more technical, editorial, or governance role.

  4. 4

    Become an Individual Supporter

    Make a donation that helps fund the Foundation's work — a direct way to back the community, whatever role you take on.

  5. 5

    Get recognized

    Active contributors are featured on their community profile with badges reflecting what they've done.
