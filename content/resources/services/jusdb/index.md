JusDB is a database reliability and DBA engineering team that provides ongoing database operations for engineering teams. For MySQL it offers remote DBA coverage, production support, performance tuning, migrations, and high-availability work on Amazon RDS, Aurora, Google Cloud SQL, Azure Flexible Server, self-managed servers, and Kubernetes. The work includes rehearsed upgrades from MySQL 5.7 and 8.0 to 8.4 LTS, online schema changes, and replication setups with ProxySQL, InnoDB Cluster, and Orchestrator. The same team covers 24 database technologies, including PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, and ClickHouse.

JusDB works with clients worldwide from offices in Bengaluru and Tiruchirappalli, with business-hours coverage Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST, and 24x7 on-call available under a service agreement. Initial-response targets are set in that agreement, and JusDB says they are targets, not resolution guarantees. Engagements start with a 30-minute scoping call, and JusDB offers a free audit of one database instance, with a mutual NDA before read-only access.
