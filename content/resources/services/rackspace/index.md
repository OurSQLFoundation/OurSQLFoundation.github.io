Rackspace Technology manages databases as part of its wider cloud operations business. For MySQL it covers the whole plan, build, and run cycle on public cloud or dedicated infrastructure: onboarding and migrations, installation, patching and upgrades, backup and restore, replication, clustered high-availability setups, capacity planning, security management, and slow-query analysis.

Each account gets a lead DBA who learns the client's data platform and runs regular health and capacity reviews, backed by a 24/7 database operations team for alerts and break-fix work. Rackspace says it deploys active-active MySQL clusters on Percona XtraDB Cluster, and it can also supply MySQL Enterprise Edition on monthly licensing.
