OptimaData is a database consultancy in Naarden, the Netherlands, working with open source and commercial platforms: MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, SQL Server, and Oracle. It describes itself as full-service — architecture design and proofs of concept, database administration and maintenance, 24/7 standby and support, remote DBA work, and on-site consultancy.

Its managed services team runs large MySQL, MariaDB, and PostgreSQL platforms in the cloud, with Galera Cluster, ClusterControl, Ansible, Kubernetes, and Grafana/Prometheus in its toolset. It also offers training and helps keep databases compliant with NIS2.
