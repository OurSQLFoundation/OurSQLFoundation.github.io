XTIVIA runs its remote database administration practice under the Virtual-DBA name. As an Oracle Partner it focuses on MySQL Enterprise Edition, and it also supports open source MySQL, MariaDB, and Percona Server.

Managed services cover on-premises environments and cloud platforms including OCI MySQL HeatWave, AWS RDS and Aurora, Google Cloud, and Azure Database for MySQL. Other work includes one-off or routine health checks, performance tuning, and designing and managing replication, clustering, and disaster recovery sites.
