Mydbops is a database services company, founded in 2015 and based in Bengaluru, India, offering remote DBA, managed DBA, consulting, and performance/security-audit services for open-source databases, with dedicated practice areas for MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, TiDB, and Cassandra.

Its managed-services offering provides 24/7 coverage with a stated incident-response SLA, a dedicated engineer and customer-success manager per client, and covers monitoring, upgrades, migrations, and performance tuning. The company is ISO and PCI-DSS certified and is listed as an AWS Advanced Tier Services Partner. For MySQL specifically, its consulting work centers on tuning, replication, and version upgrades.
