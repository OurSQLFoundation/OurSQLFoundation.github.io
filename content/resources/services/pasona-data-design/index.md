Pasona Data & Design, previously known as Smart Style, is a Japanese database company that has focused on MySQL consulting and support for domestic companies for more than twenty years. It is headquartered in Tokyo with an office in Osaka, is part of the Pasona Group, and is a certified Oracle partner for MySQL. Its site is in Japanese.

Its services cover consulting for MySQL, MariaDB, and Percona; maintenance support, including for MariaDB Enterprise and Percona; performance tuning; adoption, version upgrades such as moving to MySQL 8.x, and migrations from other databases; and monitoring and managed services. For high availability it works with MySQL replication, Group Replication, InnoDB Cluster, and NDB Cluster, as well as MariaDB Enterprise Cluster, MariaDB MaxScale, and Percona XtraDB Cluster.
