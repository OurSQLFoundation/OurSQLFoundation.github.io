Vettabase is a database consultancy founded by Federico Razzoli, working primarily with MySQL and MariaDB (alongside PostgreSQL and Cassandra). Its services include remote DBA support sold as monthly packages — a Standard tier covering business-hours consulting and a Premium tier adding 24/7 incident support — plus database health checks (remote audits covering configuration, schema design, and queries), help with major version upgrades and database-to-database migrations, deployment and configuration automation with a stated focus on MariaDB, and custom MariaDB/MySQL plugin development.

Vettabase is a MariaDB Foundation sponsor and technology partner, and became a MindsDB partner for MySQL integration in 2023. It also offers MariaDB and MySQL training courses.
