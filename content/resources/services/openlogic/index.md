OpenLogic, part of Perforce Software, sells support and services for hundreds of open source packages, databases among them. For MySQL its main offer is MySQL LTS: security backports and CVE fixes for MySQL 5.7 and 8.0 after their community end of life, delivered through a private repository, with SLA-backed 24/7/365 technical support at the Premium tier covering troubleshooting, replication, high availability, and performance tuning. It is aimed at teams that need more runway before upgrading.

OpenLogic also supports MariaDB, runs migration projects to MySQL 8.4, MariaDB, or PostgreSQL, and teaches a MySQL and PostgreSQL course in one- and two-and-a-half-day formats.
