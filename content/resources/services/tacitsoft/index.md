TacitSoft LLC is an enterprise consultancy based in Phoenix, Arizona, that describes its mission as making critical platforms easier to change and safer to run. It organizes its work into three services: platform architecture and modernization, image and OS lifecycle (secure, reproducible operating-system images across hybrid environments), and managed platform reliability and delivery. Engagements follow an assess, engineer, and govern model, and the company offers a free initial assessment report before any paid work is proposed.

TacitSoft's services are not specific to MySQL or any other database; its listed focus is cloud, Kubernetes, delivery, and OS-image engineering. It also publishes free, locally run diagnostic collectors — including a Kubernetes resource audit and a Linux compliance preflight — and package repositories for its tools.

For the database community, TacitSoft publishes [DB Daily](https://tacitsoft.dev/db-daily), a daily digest of source-linked database stories with an RSS feed. It draws on sources across engines, including Planet for the MySQL Community, and links each story to the original post.
