credativ is an open source services company based in Mönchengladbach, Germany. Its Open Source Support Center stands in for manufacturer support on community software, with guaranteed service levels, 24/7 coverage, and permanently employed engineers who work in German or English.

MariaDB is one of the databases it supports, alongside PostgreSQL, and its services range from ad-hoc support and long-term support contracts to consulting on running the software in production.
