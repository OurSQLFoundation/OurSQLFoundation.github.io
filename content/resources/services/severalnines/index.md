Severalnines, the Stockholm-founded company behind ClusterControl, sells its database-automation software with tiered support: a Standard tier included with a subscription (business-hours email support), and an optional Premium tier for production databases that adds 24/7 coverage and an uptime SLA. Architecture review, performance analysis, migration assistance, and product training sit outside the support plans and are delivered separately under a professional-services agreement.

Beyond self-hosted ClusterControl, Severalnines also runs CCX, a fully managed MySQL/MariaDB/PostgreSQL database-as-a-service built on the same ClusterControl engine, where its own team handles deployment, monitoring, and day-to-day operation of the cluster.
