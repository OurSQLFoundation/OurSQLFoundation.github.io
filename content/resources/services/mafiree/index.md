Mafiree is a database management company based in Bangalore, India, working with clients in India and abroad since 2008. It says it manages more than 10,000 database servers across MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, TiDB, ClickHouse, and other platforms.

For MySQL it offers consulting — architecture reviews, performance audits, query and InnoDB tuning — along with zero-downtime upgrades and migrations, backup and point-in-time recovery, high availability and replication, security hardening, and 24/7 DBA support with proactive monitoring. It works with self-hosted MySQL and with managed services such as Amazon RDS, Aurora, Google Cloud SQL, and Azure. It has a similar line of consulting, migration, and administration services for TiDB.
