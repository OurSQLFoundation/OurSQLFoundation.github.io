Rimini Street is a third-party enterprise software support company. For open source databases, including MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB, it offers global 24/7/365 support as an alternative to community support, for databases running on-premises, in the cloud, or in hybrid setups.

Alongside break/fix support, its open source database services include managed services, consulting, security, interoperability, and monitoring offerings, sold under its Rimini Support, Rimini Manage, Rimini Consult, Rimini Protect, Rimini Connect, and Rimini Watch names.
