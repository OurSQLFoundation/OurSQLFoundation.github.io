Northflank is a platform for deploying services, jobs, and databases, and MySQL is one of its managed add-ons next to PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Redis. Databases run on Northflank's managed cloud or, through bring-your-own-cloud, inside the customer's AWS, GCP, or Azure account, and Northflank handles patching, upgrades, and failover.

MySQL deployments scale vertically or horizontally with replicas, support creating, importing, and restoring backups, and can be paused and restarted for development and experiments. Connections use TLS with generated credentials, and the Northflank CLI can forward a database to a developer's machine. A free tier is available, with paid plans starting at a few dollars a month.
