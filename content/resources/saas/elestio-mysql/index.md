Elestio deploys and manages open source software, MySQL among it, on a cloud provider the customer picks: AWS, Azure, Hetzner, DigitalOcean, Vultr, Linode, Scaleway, Netcup, or on-premise hardware. Elestio handles installation, configuration, encryption, security, automated backups, updates, and monitoring, and instances can be resized without redeploying.

Plans have a guaranteed monthly price that covers compute, storage, and bandwidth, starting at about $11 a month, and Elestio offers a migration service for moving existing MySQL databases over. The company states that it is ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR compliant.
