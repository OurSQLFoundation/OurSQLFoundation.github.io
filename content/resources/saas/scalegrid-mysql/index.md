ScaleGrid runs and manages MySQL clusters on infrastructure it provisions in a customer's own cloud account ("Bring Your Own Cloud") across AWS, Azure, GCP, DigitalOcean, Oracle Cloud, and Akamai, or on ScaleGrid's own all-inclusive Dedicated Hosting plans; an Enterprise option covers on-premises data centers. It handles high availability, automated backups, monitoring, and version upgrades while giving customers superuser access to their MySQL instances, which fully managed DBaaS offerings from the cloud providers themselves typically restrict.

Bring Your Own Cloud plans start at a few dollars a month with usage-based scaling, and support Reserved Instance pricing for long-term cost savings on top of the underlying cloud provider's compute.
