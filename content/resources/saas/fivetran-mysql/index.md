Fivetran's MySQL connector is a fully managed data integration that extracts data from MySQL and replicates it to a destination of choice, such as Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Azure Synapse, or an S3 data lake. MySQL is also available as a Fivetran destination.

Fivetran offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card required and a free plan for small use cases; continued syncing beyond those requires a paid plan.
