Civo, a cloud provider built around Kubernetes, runs managed MySQL and PostgreSQL databases. Civo handles provisioning, patching, backups, failure detection, and recovery; customers choose how many replicas to provision for high availability and pick from instance sizes billed by the hour.

Pricing covers only the RAM, CPU, NVMe storage, and extra replica nodes selected: setup, backups, and 24/7 support are included, and ingress and egress traffic is free. Databases can be created from the dashboard, CLI, or API, and Civo offers free credit to get started.
