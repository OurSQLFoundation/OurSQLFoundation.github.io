FOSDEM (Free and Open Source Software Developers' European Meeting) is a free, non-commercial, volunteer-organized conference for open source developers, held annually at the ULB Solbosch campus in Brussels and organized by FOSDEM VZW, a Belgian nonprofit. Attendance is free and registration is not required.
