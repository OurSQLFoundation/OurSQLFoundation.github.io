ConFoo Montreal 2027 is a three-day full-stack software development conference held on **February 24–26, 2027** at the Hotel Bonaventure in Montreal. International speakers give practical presentations on databases, application architecture, performance, security, cloud computing, DevOps, testing, and programming languages.
