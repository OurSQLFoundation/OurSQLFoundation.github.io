DevOps Pro Europe moves to Riga for its ninth edition. Workshops are on **May 25, 2027** at the National Library of Latvia, and the conference runs **May 26–28** at Apollo Kino Akropole. Topics include AIOps, MLOps, GitOps, serverless, DevSecOps, and Kubernetes. The MariaDB Foundation lists the event in its calendar.
