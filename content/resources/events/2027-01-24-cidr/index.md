CIDR (Conference on Innovative Data Systems Research) is a systems-oriented database research conference, complementary to mainstream venues like SIGMOD and VLDB, welcoming papers on innovative system architecture ideas, systems-building experience, and experimental studies, for researchers and practitioners from academia and industry. The 2027 edition is held at the Mövenpick Hotel Amsterdam City Centre.
