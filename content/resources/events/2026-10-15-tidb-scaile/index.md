TiDB SCaiLE is a one-day conference organized by PingCAP, maker of the open-source distributed SQL database TiDB, focused on data infrastructure for AI-native and agentic workloads. The 2026 US edition at the Computer History Museum runs two concurrent tracks — "AI-Native: Infinite Agents" and "Enterprise: Infinite Data" — with practitioner-led sessions from engineering, data, and AI-infrastructure teams running TiDB in production.
