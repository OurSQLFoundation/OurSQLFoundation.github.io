The FOSSASIA Summit 2027 runs **March 7–9, 2027** at True Digital Park West in Bangkok, with the main conference on March 8–9. The summit features technical talks, workshops, project showcases, and community sessions on cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes, infrastructure as code, cybersecurity, observability, database operations, scalability, and high availability. Selected sessions are also available online.
