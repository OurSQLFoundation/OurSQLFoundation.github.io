A free online session from the OurSQL Foundation, on **November 3, 2026, at 10:00 AM ET / 16:00 CET**. [René Cannaò](/resources/member-directory/people/rene-cannao/) (CEO) and [Alkin Tezuysal](/resources/member-directory/people/alkin-tezuysal/) (Chief Strategy Officer) of ProxySQL walk through how the ecosystem has grown beyond a proxy: MySQL and PostgreSQL support, the expansion toward DuckDB, deeper integration with cloud platforms and managed environments, and where related projects like Orchestrator and dbdeployer fit across the database lifecycle — tied together through real operational problems rather than a feature list.

Full details and registration are on the [webinar page](/community/webinars/beyond-the-proxy-proxysql-ecosystem/).
