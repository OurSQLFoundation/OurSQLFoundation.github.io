KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026, the Cloud Native Computing Foundation's flagship conference, runs **November 9–12, 2026** in Salt Lake City, Utah, mostly at the Salt Palace Convention Center. It brings together maintainers, developers, and infrastructure teams for sessions on Kubernetes, platform engineering, observability, security, and production operations — relevant to anyone running MySQL on Kubernetes. November 9 is reserved for pre-event and co-located programming; the main conference follows on November 10–12.
