A free online session from the OurSQL Foundation, on **November 24, 2026, at 10:00 AM ET / 16:00 CET**. Kate Obiidykhata, General Manager for Cloud-Native Solutions at Percona, introduces Autopilot Mode in the [Percona Operator for MySQL](/resources/software/percona-operator-for-mysql/): the operator derives a production-grade MySQL configuration from each cluster's CPU, memory, and workload profile, and re-tunes it automatically whenever resources change.

Full details and registration are on the [webinar page](/community/webinars/autopilot-mysql-kubernetes-operator/).
