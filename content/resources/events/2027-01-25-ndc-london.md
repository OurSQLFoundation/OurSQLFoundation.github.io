NDC London 2027 is a five-day event for software developers, held on **January 25–29, 2027** at the Queen Elizabeth II Centre in London. It combines practical workshops with conference sessions (110 sessions in total) covering application architecture, cloud infrastructure, security, testing, and database-related development practices.
