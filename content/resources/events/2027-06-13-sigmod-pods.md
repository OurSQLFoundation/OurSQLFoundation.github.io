The annual ACM SIGMOD/PODS conference is a leading international forum for database researchers, practitioners, developers, and users, with research and industrial talks, tutorials, demonstrations, and workshops. The 2027 edition runs **June 13–18, 2027** at the Hyatt Regency Huntington Beach Resort and Spa.
