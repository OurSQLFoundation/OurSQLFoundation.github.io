A free online session from the OurSQL Foundation, on **October 15, 2026, at 10:00 AM ET / 16:00 CEST**. [Daniel Guzmán Burgos](/resources/member-directory/people/daniel-guzman-burgos/), the creator of [dbtrail](/resources/software/dbtrail/), shows in a mostly live demo — on a real schema, with a real mistake and a real recovery — how to identify which rows changed and when, inspect before-and-after images, query any table as of a past timestamp with plain SQL, and generate the reversal statements, including changes propagated through foreign-key cascades. Everything shown is reproducible on a test instance.

Full details and registration are on the [webinar page](/community/webinars/mysql-time-travel-row-level-recovery/).
