PGConf.EU is PostgreSQL Europe's 16th annual PostgreSQL Conference Europe, described by its organizers as by far the largest PostgreSQL conference in Europe, with multiple parallel tracks spanning database internals to end-user case studies for DBAs, developers, decision-makers, and contributors. The main program runs October 20–22, with October 23 set aside as a community-organized "Community Events Day."
