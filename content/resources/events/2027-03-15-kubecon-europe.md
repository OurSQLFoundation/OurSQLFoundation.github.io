KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2027 runs **March 15–18, 2027** in Barcelona, Spain. The Cloud Native Computing Foundation's flagship European conference brings together cloud-native adopters, maintainers, and technologists for four days of co-located events, technical sessions, and project collaboration on platform engineering, observability, security, and cloud and AI infrastructure.
