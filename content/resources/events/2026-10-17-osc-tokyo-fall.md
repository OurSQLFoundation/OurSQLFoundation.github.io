Open Source Conference (OSC) 2026 Tokyo/Fall is a free one-day exhibition on **October 17, 2026 (10:00–16:00)** at the Tokyo Metropolitan Industrial Trade Center, Taito Building (6th floor), about a five-minute walk from Asakusa Station. Open source communities, companies, and organizations present their projects, and a stage program with presentations starts at 11:00 and is streamed live on YouTube. It is run by the Open Source Conference Executive Committee.

MySQL will be part of the event: according to the [MySQL events listing](https://planet.mysql.com/events/), visitors can stop by the MySQL booth to meet the team and talk about MySQL, the community, and their projects.
