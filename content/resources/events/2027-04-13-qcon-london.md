QCon London 2027 is a conference for experienced software engineers and architects, held at the QEII Centre in Westminster. The three-day conference runs **April 13–15, 2027**, with 75+ practitioner speakers across 15 engineering tracks including distributed-system debugging, observability, resilient architecture, data platforms, security, and performance engineering. An optional InfoQ Certified Architect program follows on April 16.
