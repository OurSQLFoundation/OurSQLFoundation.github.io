The Swedish MySQL User Group meets in Gothenburg on **October 28, 2026** for an evening of MySQL updates, technical content, discussion, and networking. Mario Beck, Director of MySQL Solution Engineering EMEA at Oracle, will present *MySQL Latest News* and *Extending MySQL Shell*, and light refreshments and drinks will be available during the event.

Details are announced through the user group's [LinkedIn group](https://www.linkedin.com/groups/4510296/) and the [MySQL events listing](https://planet.mysql.com/events/).
