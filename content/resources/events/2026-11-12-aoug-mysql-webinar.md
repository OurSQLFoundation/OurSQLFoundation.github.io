The Austrian Oracle User Group (AOUG) hosts an online MySQL webinar on **November 12, 2026**, with two sessions by MySQL's Scott Stroz exploring the community's plans for open innovation and the new features in MySQL 9.7 LTS. The webinar offers community updates and practical technical insights for developers, DBAs, architects, and open source contributors.

Session details are from the [MySQL events listing](https://planet.mysql.com/events/); registration and the schedule are on the [AOUG event page](https://aoug.at/ords/r/web/aoug/veranstaltung?p41_ver_id=711&cs=1vgtEYHe8cG2hiO8aG8wQWqsyLl5G-tibPaK4pVDjFOe54tqxZ58jPka47LpoArDJoJo5oJRPf30BiIq27E2iTQ).
