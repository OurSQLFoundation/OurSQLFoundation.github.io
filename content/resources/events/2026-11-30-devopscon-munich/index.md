DevOpsCon Munich 2026 describes itself as the conference for CI/CD, Kubernetes, platform engineering, and DevSecOps. It runs **November 30 – December 4, 2026** at the Holiday Inn Munich – City Centre, and can also be followed online with a livestream and Q&A access. The conference days are December 1–2 (with an AI Platform Engineering Day on November 30), and the program is organized in tracks such as Kubernetes & Cloud Native, CI/CD, DevSecOps, Observability & Reliability, and a Platform Engineering Summit. Workshops run on November 30 and December 3, and two-day bootcamps on December 3–4.
