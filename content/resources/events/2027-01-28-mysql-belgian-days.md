MySQL Belgian Days 2027 is a two-day conference for MySQL developers, DBAs, engineers, and users, held on **January 28–29, 2027** at the ICAB Incubator in Brussels, right before FOSDEM. Sessions cover MySQL performance and optimization, high availability, application development, observability, and recent developments in MySQL Community Edition and open-source collaboration, with plenty of time to meet MySQL engineers and community members. The organizers accept community talk proposals.
