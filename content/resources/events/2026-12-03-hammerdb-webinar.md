A free online session from the OurSQL Foundation, on **December 3, 2026, at 10:00 AM ET / 16:00 CET**. [Steve Shaw](/resources/member-directory/people/steve-shaw/), the creator of [HammerDB](/resources/software/hammerdb/), shows how an open, standard benchmark suite hosted by the TPC-OSS initiative makes database performance results comparable and reproducible across MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, Oracle, SQL Server, and Db2, and walks through what's new in HammerDB v6.0.

Full details and registration are on the [webinar page](/community/webinars/fastest-database-hammerdb/).
