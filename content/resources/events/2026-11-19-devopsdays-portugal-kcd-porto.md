DevOpsDays Portugal × KCD Porto 2026 is a joint DevOpsDays and Kubernetes Community Days conference held on **November 19–20, 2026** at the Super Bock Arena (Pavilhão Rosa Mota) in the Jardins do Palácio de Cristal, Porto. It features technical talks, keynotes, and practical sessions on Kubernetes, platform engineering, cloud-native infrastructure, and software delivery, for developers, operations engineers, SREs, and platform teams.
