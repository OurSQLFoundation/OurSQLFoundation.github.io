On the third Thursday of the month (unless otherwise specified), the Vitess community meets by video conference at 8:00 a.m. PST. Everyone is welcome, regardless of location or familiarity with Vitess. Meetings are listed on the [CNCF Public Events calendar](https://www.cncf.io/community/calendar/), and notes and agenda items from past meetings are kept in a [shared document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XLUdKePtj9aZD0E2Nlr3VRk1NIaSBgeIvXUj13y5CFk/edit).

To propose a topic, email the maintainers or reply to the call for agenda items on the [Vitess Slack](/resources/community/vitess-slack/) a few days before the call.
