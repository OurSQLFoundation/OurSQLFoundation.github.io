Henrik Ingo is MySQL Community Architect at Oracle. He has been active in the open source database community for about 20 years: he has worked at MySQL, MariaDB, Galera Cluster, MongoDB, and DataStax, and recently returned to the Oracle MySQL team.

He is also the author of *Open Life: The Philosophy of Open Source*.
