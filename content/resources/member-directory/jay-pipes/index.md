Jay Pipes is the co-author, with Michael Kruckenberg, of [*Pro MySQL*](/resources/books/pro-mysql/) (Apress, 2005). At the time of the book he was the North American community relations manager at MySQL. He had written articles for Linux Magazine and regularly helped software developers make effective use of MySQL.
