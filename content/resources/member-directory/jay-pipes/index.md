Jay Pipes is the co-author, with Michael Kruckenberg, of [*Pro MySQL*](/resources/books/pro-mysql/) (Apress, 2005). At the time of the book he was the North American community relations manager at MySQL. He had written articles for Linux Magazine and regularly helped software developers make effective use of MySQL, and he had given sessions on performance tuning at the MySQL Users Conference, RedHat Summit, NY PHP Conference, OSCON, and Ohio LinuxFest, among others.
