Roman Nozdrin is an engineer at MariaDB who works on [ColumnStore](/resources/software/mariadb-columnstore/), the columnar storage engine for MariaDB Server. He mentored ColumnStore projects in MariaDB's Google Summer of Code 2022, and at MariaDB Fest 2025 in Brussels he gave the talk *Ascend to New Heights: Discover What ColumnStore can Really Do for You*, where he was listed as Principal Software Engineer, ColumnStore.

At [DuckDB × MySQL Day](/events/duckdb-mysql-day/) he presents *When the Sea Lion Learns to Quack: DuckDB as a MariaDB Storage Engine*.
