Evgeniy Patlan is a build and release engineer at Percona, where he has worked since April 2016. Before Percona he spent more than eight years at PortaOne, moving from support engineer to build/release engineer. On the Percona blog he writes about package repositories and builds, Valkey packages, and benchmarks, including [running DuckDB as a MySQL 9.7 storage engine](https://www.percona.com/blog/running-duckdb-as-a-mysql-9-7-storage-engine/) and [the DuckDB MySQL engine at 500 GB](https://www.percona.com/blog/the-duckdb-mysql-engine-at-500-gb/).

At [DuckDB × MySQL Day](/events/duckdb-mysql-day/) he presents *Duck Hunt: An Independent Benchmark of DuckDB-Powered MySQL*.
