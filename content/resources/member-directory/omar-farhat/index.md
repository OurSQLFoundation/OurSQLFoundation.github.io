Omar Farhat is a software engineer on the [Amazon Aurora MySQL](/resources/saas/amazon-aurora-mysql/) team at AWS, which he joined in 2020, and specializes in MySQL logical replication. He first worked with MySQL 5.6 during his master's studies in databases and distributed systems at the University of Waterloo. His team's upstream work includes the [large-transaction binary log optimization](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/database/aws-contributes-a-large-transaction-binary-log-optimization-to-mysql/) that AWS contributed to MySQL together with Oracle, and he is working on an in-memory relay log for replicas that avoids writing received transactions to disk and reading them back. He took part in the MySQL Contributor Summit in August 2026.
