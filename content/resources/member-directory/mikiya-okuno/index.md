Mikiya Okuno works in MySQL support and is a long-time advocate of free software. He is the author of two MySQL books in Japanese published by Gijutsu-Hyoronsha: *MySQL Cluster構築・運用バイブル* (2012), the first Japanese-language guide to MySQL Cluster, and *エキスパートのためのMySQL［運用+管理］トラブルシューティングガイド*.

With Jesper Wisborg Krogh he co-wrote [*Pro MySQL NDB Cluster*](/resources/books/pro-mysql-ndb-cluster/) (Apress, 2017).
