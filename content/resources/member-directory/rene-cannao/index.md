René Cannaò is the creator of ProxySQL and CEO of ProxySQL LLC, the company behind the project. He is a database expert specializing in MySQL performance optimization, query routing, and high availability, with years of experience running large-scale MySQL infrastructure before building ProxySQL to solve the connection and traffic-management problems he saw firsthand.
