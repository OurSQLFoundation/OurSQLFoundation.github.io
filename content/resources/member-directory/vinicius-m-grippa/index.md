Vinicius Grippa is Lead Database Engineer at Readyset, a caching layer for MySQL and Postgres, and an Oracle ACE Director. He spent years in Percona Support, and is the creator of [myflames](/resources/software/myflames/), which turns MySQL and MariaDB query plans into interactive charts. With Sergey Kuzmichev he co-authored [*Learning MySQL*, 2nd Edition](/resources/books/learning-mysql/) (O'Reilly, 2021). He helps run the MySQL community in Brazil. [Read his interview in People Behind MySQL](/blog/vinicius-grippa-interview/).
