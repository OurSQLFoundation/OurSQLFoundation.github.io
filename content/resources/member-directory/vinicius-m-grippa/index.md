Vinicius Grippa is a co-author, with Sergey Kuzmichev, of [*Learning MySQL*, 2nd Edition](/resources/books/learning-mysql/) (O'Reilly, 2021) and the creator of [myflames](/resources/software/myflames/), which turns MySQL and MariaDB query plans into interactive charts. At the time of the book he was a senior support engineer at Percona and an Oracle ACE Associate, with a bachelor's degree in computer science and 13 years of experience with databases, and had become a specialist in the MySQL and MongoDB ecosystems. He is active in the open source community, including Slack communities, meetups, and conferences in Europe, Asia, and North and South America.
