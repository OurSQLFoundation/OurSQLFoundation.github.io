Kenny Gryp is a MySQL specialist best known for his work on high availability and replication. At Percona he was a long-time consultant and MySQL Practice Manager, writing extensively on the Percona blog about Galera, Percona XtraDB Cluster, and MySQL operations. He later joined Oracle's MySQL team, working in product management on [MySQL Server](/resources/software/mysql-server/) with a focus on InnoDB Cluster and high-availability features. He is a regular speaker at Percona Live, FOSDEM, and other MySQL events.
