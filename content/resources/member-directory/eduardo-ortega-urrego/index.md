Eduardo Ortega Urrego is a Staff Software Engineer on Slack's Datastores team. Trained as a physicist, he has spent much of the past decade operating large database fleets, first at Booking.com and now at Slack.

In his [OurSQL Foundation webinar](/community/webinars/mysql-vitess-slack/), Eduardo explains how Slack automates operations across thousands of MySQL and [Vitess](/resources/software/vitess/) instances and helps clients respond to database overload.
