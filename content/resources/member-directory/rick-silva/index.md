Rick Silva is a software developer with decades of database experience. He has worked at Harvard Business School, Zipcar, and various financial services companies. He is the author of [*MySQL Crash Course: A Hands-on Introduction to Database Development*](/resources/books/mysql-crash-course/) (No Starch Press, 2023). The scripts for the book's example databases and exercises are published in the author's GitHub repository.
