Heather VanCura is Vice President of External Standards & Community Engagement at Oracle. She leads the MySQL Community Outreach team and chairs the [MySQL Community Steering Committee](https://dev.mysql.com/community/steering-committee/), whose members also include representatives from AWS and Google Cloud. She also leads the Java Community Process (JCP) program.
