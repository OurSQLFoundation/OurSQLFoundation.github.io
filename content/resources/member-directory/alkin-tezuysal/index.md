Alkin Tezuysal is Chief Strategy Officer at ProxySQL and an open source database leader with more than 30 years of experience in database architecture, operations, services, and community development. He has led global teams supporting MySQL and ClickHouse users across a wide range of industries. Alkin is an Oracle ACE Pro and co-author of *MySQL Cookbook, 4th Edition*. He was also recognized among the Redgate 100's most influential people in the database community.
