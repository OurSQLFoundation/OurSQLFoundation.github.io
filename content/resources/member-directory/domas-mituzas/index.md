Domas Mituzas is a database and performance engineer known for running MySQL at some of the largest sites on the web. He has been involved with Wikipedia's technology since 2004 as a Wikimedia systems administrator and developer, and served on the Wikimedia Foundation Board of Trustees. After working as a MySQL performance specialist at MySQL AB and Sun, he joined Facebook's database engineering team, where he became a well-known voice for scaling sharded MySQL. He is the original author of [mydumper](/resources/software/mydumper/), the parallel logical backup tool.
