Shlomi Noach is a database engineer and the author of several of the most widely used open source tools for operating MySQL at scale. He created [orchestrator](/resources/software/orchestrator/), a replication topology manager and high-availability tool, and while at GitHub built [gh-ost](/resources/software/gh-ost/), the triggerless online schema migration tool, and [freno](/resources/software/freno/), a cooperative throttling service. At PlanetScale he is a maintainer of [Vitess](/resources/software/vitess/), where he has led work on online schema migrations and VTOrc. He was named a MySQL Legend in 2024 and is a regular speaker at FOSDEM and Percona Live.
