Marco Tusa is a MySQL consultant with decades of experience in database architecture and high availability. He worked as a MySQL lead at Pythian before joining [Percona](/resources/services/percona/) in 2013, where he managed the Consulting Rapid Response Team and later led the High Availability practice. He has written widely about [ProxySQL](/resources/software/proxysql/), MySQL proxies, and Galera-based clusters.
