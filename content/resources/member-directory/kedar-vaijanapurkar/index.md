Kedar Vaijanapurkar is MySQL DBA II at Percona, where he works with production database environments and managed services customers. His expertise includes MySQL administration, performance optimization, high availability, observability, cloud migrations, and complex troubleshooting. Kedar is also the creator of MySQL Advisor and database test-data generation tools. Through his technical blog and *Deep Dive MySQL* podcast, he shares practical guidance on MySQL operations, upgrades, performance, Percona XtraDB Cluster, ProxySQL, and related open source technologies.
