Timour Katchaounov is a database engineer who worked on the query optimizer at MySQL AB and later at Monty Program Ab on MariaDB, with a focus on subquery optimization. With Sergei Petrunia he presented optimizer tuning sessions at Percona Live 2012 and the 2013 MySQL Conference, and he spoke on subqueries at the 2011 O'Reilly MySQL Conference.
