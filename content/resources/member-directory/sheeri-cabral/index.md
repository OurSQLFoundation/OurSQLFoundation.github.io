Sheeri Cabral is a database administrator, author, and one of the most recognized community builders in MySQL's history. Together with Keith Murphy, Sheeri wrote *MySQL Administrator's Bible* (Wiley, 2009), a comprehensive guide for new MySQL DBAs and for DBAs coming from other database platforms. Sheeri founded and organized the Boston MySQL User Group and produced *OurSQL: The MySQL Database Podcast*, and was named MySQL Community Member of the Year in both 2007 and 2008. Sheeri has also led database teams at Pythian and spoken at many MySQL conferences.
