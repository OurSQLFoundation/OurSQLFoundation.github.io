Sveta Smirnova is a co-author, with [Alkin Tezuysal](/resources/member-directory/people/alkin-tezuysal/), of [*MySQL Cookbook*, 4th Edition](/resources/books/mysql-cookbook/) (O'Reilly, 2022) and the author of *MySQL Troubleshooting*. At the time of the book she was a principal support escalation specialist at Percona. Her main professional interests include problem-solving, working with tricky issues, and teaching others how to deal effectively with MySQL problems, bugs, and gotchas. She has spoken at many events, including FOSDEM, Percona Live, and Oracle OpenWorld.
