Johan Andersson is co-founder and CTO of [Severalnines](/resources/services/severalnines/) and the creator of [ClusterControl](/resources/software/clustercontrol/), its database deployment and management platform. Before founding Severalnines he was a Principal Consultant at MySQL, Sun Microsystems, and Oracle, where he led the MySQL Clustering & High Availability consulting group and was one of the leading experts on MySQL NDB Cluster.
