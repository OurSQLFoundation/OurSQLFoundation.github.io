Miguel Araújo works on high availability, replication, and scalability on Oracle’s MySQL team. He leads development of AdminAPI in [MySQL Shell](/resources/software/mysql-shell/), which helps users deploy and manage InnoDB Cluster, InnoDB ReplicaSet, and InnoDB ClusterSet. He also leads development of the MySQL REST Service component. [Oracle MySQL blog](https://blogs.oracle.com/mysql/introducing-the-mysql-rest-service-component-now-generally-available)
