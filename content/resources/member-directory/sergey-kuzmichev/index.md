Sergey Kuzmichev is a co-author, with Vinicius Grippa, of [*Learning MySQL*, 2nd Edition](/resources/books/learning-mysql/) (O'Reilly, 2021). At the time of the book he was a senior support engineer at Percona; before that he was a DBA and DevOps engineer for nearly a decade. He enjoys solving technical mysteries and building reliable systems, contributes code and bug reports to open source projects, and writes blog posts about MySQL and other open source databases.
