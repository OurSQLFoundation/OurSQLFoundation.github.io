Silvia Botros is the co-author, with Jeremy Tinley, of [*High Performance MySQL*, 4th Edition](/resources/books/high-performance-mysql-4th/) (O'Reilly, 2021). At the time of publication she was a Software Architect at Twilio. During her time at SendGrid, she helped build the database platform that supports sending billions of emails, supported other products, and drove datastore designs from inception to production.
