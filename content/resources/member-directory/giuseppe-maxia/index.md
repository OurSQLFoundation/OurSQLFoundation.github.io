Giuseppe Maxia, known in the community as "The Data Charmer", is a long-time MySQL developer, trainer, and speaker. He created MySQL-Sandbox and its successor [dbdeployer](/resources/software/dbdeployer/), tools that let DBAs and developers spin up single servers, replication setups, and Group Replication clusters on a laptop in seconds, and which became a standard part of the MySQL testing toolkit. He served as MySQL Community Team Lead at MySQL AB, Sun, and Oracle, and later worked at VMware. He archived dbdeployer in 2023; the project has since been revived by the community with contributions from ProxySQL and MariaDB.
