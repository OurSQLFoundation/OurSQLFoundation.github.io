Ronald Bradford is a database architect and consultant whose relational database career began in 1989 with Ingres and Oracle. He is the author of the Oracle Press *Effective MySQL* series, including *Optimizing SQL Statements* (2011), *Backup and Recovery* (2012), and *Replication Techniques in Depth*, co-written with Chris Schneider. An Oracle ACE Director and a recipient of the MySQL Community Member of the Year award, he has been one of the most prolific MySQL bloggers and a frequent speaker at MySQL conferences and user groups.
