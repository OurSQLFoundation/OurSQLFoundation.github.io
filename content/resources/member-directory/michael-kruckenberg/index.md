Michael Kruckenberg is the co-author, with [Jay Pipes](/resources/member-directory/people/jay-pipes/), of [*Pro MySQL*](/resources/books/pro-mysql/) (Apress, 2005), an advanced, developer-focused guide to MySQL's transaction processing, indexing, query optimization, stored procedures, replication, and internal source architecture.
