*MariaDB High Performance* is written for DBAs and architects who already know MariaDB or MySQL. It covers performance analysis and optimization, replication and its monitoring, multi-master setups with Galera Cluster, load balancing in front of the cluster, and sharding data across servers with the Spider storage engine.

Published by Packt in 2014, at about 300 pages.
