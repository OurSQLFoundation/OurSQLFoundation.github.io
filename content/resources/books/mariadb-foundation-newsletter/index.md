The MariaDB Foundation Newsletter is the non-profit Foundation's own periodic update — distinct from MariaDB plc's separate commercial newsletter — covering Foundation governance, MariaDB Server releases, and community news. Issues are published as regular posts on mariadb.org and archived under its "Newsletter" tag.

The September 2026 issue, for example, covered the Foundation publishing a governance framework for MariaDB Server, the return of contribution-statistics reporting after a gap of over a year, and that quarter's round of maintenance releases across the supported LTS series — the first round without 10.6. It's free to read directly on mariadb.org.
