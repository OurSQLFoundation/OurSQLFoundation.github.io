*Mastering MariaDB* covers advanced techniques for managing, optimizing, and maintaining MariaDB servers. After an overview of MariaDB essentials, it works through debugging, optimizing queries, transactions and locks, users and connections, caches, InnoDB compressed tables, backup and disaster recovery, replication, table partitioning, data sharding, and MariaDB Galera Cluster.

The author, Federico Razzoli, later founded the consultancy [Vettabase](/resources/services/vettabase/). Published by Packt in 2014, at about 380 pages.
