Outerbase Studio started as a GUI for LibSQL and SQLite and has grown to cover other databases. MySQL support is labelled beta with limited features, and it goes through Outerbase Studio Desktop, a lightweight Electron wrapper that enables drivers a browser environment can't run.

It's written in TypeScript and released under the AGPLv3.
