DbSchema reverse-engineers databases into ER diagrams and documentation, supports designing schemas offline, and lets teams review schema changes in Git before deploying them. MySQL and MariaDB are among its supported databases.

The Community Edition is free for non-commercial use, and paid plans add features, with a 15-day Pro trial.
