ChunJun synchronizes and transforms data between heterogeneous sources on top of Flink, configured through JSON templates or Flink SQL scripts. It abstracts databases into reader, writer, and lookup plugins, and MySQL is among the supported sources and targets. It was known as FlinkX until February 2022.

It's released under the Apache 2.0 license.
