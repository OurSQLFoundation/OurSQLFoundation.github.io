ToolJet builds operational apps on top of existing databases, APIs, and SaaS systems, either visually or by prompting its AI builder, and MySQL is among its data source plugins. It can be self-hosted or used as ToolJet Cloud.

The open-source edition is released under the AGPLv3, and ToolJet Solutions also offers commercial editions.
