The `ansible.mysql` collection is part of the Ansible package and automates MySQL-related tasks from playbooks. All content from `community.mysql` moved here when that collection was deprecated, and MariaDB automation now lives in a separate `ansible.mariadb` collection.

It's released under the GPLv3 or later.
