MyXQL encodes and decodes Elixir values to and from MySQL's text and binary protocols and supports transactions, prepared queries, streaming, and pooling through DBConnection. It supports the `mysql_native_password`, `sha256_password`, and `caching_sha2_password` authentication methods, and it's the driver Ecto uses for MySQL.

It's released under the Apache 2.0 license.
