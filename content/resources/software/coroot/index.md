Coroot gathers metrics, logs, traces, and profiles automatically with eBPF and turns them into insights such as root cause analysis. For MySQL, it observes queries between applications and the database with no extra integration, then adds statistics from the MySQL Performance Schema to explain what happens inside the server.

It's released under the Apache 2.0 license, and Coroot also sells a commercial edition.
