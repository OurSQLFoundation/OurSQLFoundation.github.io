DBCanvas lets you design a database topology on a canvas — picking from eleven starter templates covering Percona XtraDB Cluster, Percona Server, MySQL Community with asynchronous replication, InnoDB Cluster/Group Replication, and MariaDB (standalone, replication, or Galera) — then deploy it with one click into real, running Docker containers wired together with DNS, TLS, LDAP, replication, monitoring, and backups. It's built for testing, demos, training, troubleshooting, and benchmarking: spin up a production-shaped cluster in minutes, exercise it, and tear it down.

**DBCanvas is explicitly for previewing, testing, and learning — not for production.** Everything it deploys is disposable lab infrastructure, with default credentials and containers that trade durability for speed of setup.
