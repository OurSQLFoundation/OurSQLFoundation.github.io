SQLPage builds data-centric web apps from plain `.sql` files: each query selects, inserts, updates, or deletes data, and the results render as tables, lists, charts, and forms. MySQL is one of its built-in drivers, and compatible databases such as MariaDB and TiDB work too.

It's written in Rust and released under the MIT license.
