NocoDB gives teams a spreadsheet-like interface for building and sharing databases, and it can connect to external databases such as MySQL. It can be self-hosted or used as a hosted service.

Its source is public, but it's licensed under the Sustainable Use License rather than an OSI-approved open-source license.
