DataGrip connects to, queries, and manages many databases in one interface, including MySQL and MariaDB, and runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux. It also lets AI agents work with databases through your own API key, an existing subscription, or JetBrains AI.

DataGrip is free for non-commercial use. Commercial use requires a subscription, with a 30-day free trial.
