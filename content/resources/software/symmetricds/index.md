SymmetricDS is platform-independent, web-enabled, and database-agnostic, built to synchronize data across sites with conflict resolution and tolerance for limited bandwidth, intermittent connectivity, and firewalls.

The open-source edition is released under the AGPLv3, and JumpMind sells SymmetricDS Pro with additional enterprise capabilities.
