TablePlus is a native client for querying, editing, and managing databases, with apps for macOS, Windows, Linux, and iPhone and iPad. It supports MySQL, MariaDB, SingleStore, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, SQLite, Redis, MongoDB, and others.

It's sold as a one-time perpetual license. A free trial is limited to two open tabs, two windows, and two advanced filters.
