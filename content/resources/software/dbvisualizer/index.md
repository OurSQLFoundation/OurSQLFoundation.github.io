DbVisualizer connects to the most popular databases, including MySQL, and includes support for database-specific object types. The free edition covers the standard feature set, and Pro features are enabled with a license key in the same installation.

Pro subscriptions include a perpetual usage license, and a 21-day free trial unlocks all Pro features.
