The Atlas Operator brings [Atlas](/resources/software/atlas/) schema management to Kubernetes: you describe the desired schema in Atlas DDL or plain SQL as a custom resource, and the operator plans and applies the migration. Supported databases include MySQL and MariaDB.

It's released under the Apache 2.0 license.
