Valentina Studio manages MySQL and MariaDB alongside DuckDB, MongoDB, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, SQLite, and Valentina DB, with features such as a diagram editor that designs databases and generates objects from the diagram.

The free edition is available on Linux, macOS, and Windows, and paid editions add further features.
