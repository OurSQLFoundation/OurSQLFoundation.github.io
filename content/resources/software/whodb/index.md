WhoDB gives you one place to explore a database, edit data, run queries, and understand how a schema fits together, without installing a heavyweight client. It supports MySQL, MariaDB, and TiDB among other databases. AI features are optional and can connect to local models through Ollama or to hosted providers.

It's written in Go and released under the Apache 2.0 license.
