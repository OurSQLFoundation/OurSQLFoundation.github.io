Querious is a MySQL client built specifically for the Mac, designed to look and behave like a native macOS app. Querious 4 is the current release and requires macOS 12 or later, and an early release of Querious 5 adds PostgreSQL support.

It's sold as a one-time purchase rather than a subscription, with a free trial.
