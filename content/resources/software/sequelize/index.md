Sequelize provides a model-based, promise-driven ORM layer for Node.js, with solid transaction support, associations and eager/lazy loading, migrations, and read-replication support for scaling reads across multiple MySQL servers. Its MySQL and MariaDB dialect is built on the `mysql2` driver under the hood, alongside separate dialect packages for Postgres, SQLite, SQL Server, Snowflake, Oracle, and Db2.

It's released under the MIT license and has been a fixture of the Node.js data-access ecosystem since 2014, maintained by the Sequelize community through an Open Collective-funded core team.
