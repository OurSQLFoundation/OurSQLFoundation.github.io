Appsmith builds internal applications such as dashboards, admin panels, and customer 360 views on top of existing data sources. MySQL is supported through a dedicated plugin, so tables can be bound to widgets and CRUD pages generated from them.

It can be self-hosted with Docker or Kubernetes or used through Appsmith Cloud, and it's released under the Apache 2.0 license.
