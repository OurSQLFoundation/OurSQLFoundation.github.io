SQLE governs SQL quality across the development, testing, release, and production stages, checking statements against audit rules before and after they reach the database. MySQL is covered by its native audit plugin, while separate plugins extend it to PostgreSQL, Oracle, SQL Server, and DB2, along with databases such as OceanBase and TDSQL.

It's developed by Shanghai ActionTech (爱可生) and released under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. ActionTech also offers an Enterprise Edition and paid support.
