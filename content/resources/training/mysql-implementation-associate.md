The MySQL Implementation Associate exam (1Z0-922) is Oracle's official MySQL certification, aimed at technical professionals and administrators who install, configure, and manage MySQL — on-premises and in the cloud. It validates installation-preparation and database-design skills, high availability and replication, enterprise security, backup and restore, monitoring, and a basic understanding of HeatWave.

It's delivered as a proctored exam through Oracle University / MyLearn, listed at $245 USD. Oracle provides a matching "Become a MySQL Implementation Associate" learning path and a practice exam on the MyLearn platform to prepare candidates before they sit the real exam.
