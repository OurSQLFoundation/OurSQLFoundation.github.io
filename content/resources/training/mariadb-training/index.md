MariaDB Corporation runs its own instructor-led training program for MariaDB database administration and development, delivered in three formats: regularly scheduled Public Online Training sessions, Private Online Training booked for a single company, and On-Site Training held at the customer's own offices.

The catalog includes DBA-track courses — such as the Standard DBA course MariaDB recommends completing (or having equivalent hands-on experience) before sitting its Certified Database Administrator Associate exam — and developer-track courses covering application development against MariaDB from Java, Node.js, Python, PHP, Perl, C/C++, and .NET. Course schedules and the full catalog are published on MariaDB's own training page.
