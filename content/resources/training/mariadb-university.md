MariaDB University is the official learning platform from MariaDB. Its catalog includes Enterprise Server Administrator and Advanced Administrator courses for versions 10.6 and 11.4, along with courses on MariaDB MaxScale and the MariaDB Enterprise Kubernetes Operator.

Courses are listed at $150 each, and the matching assessments can be bought separately for $100.
