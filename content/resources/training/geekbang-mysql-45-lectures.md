The column has a foundations module on MySQL architecture, logging, transaction isolation, and locking, followed by a practice module that works through common production problems such as slow single-row queries and replication lag. It includes more than 100 hand-drawn diagrams.

The author, Lin Xiaobin, is a former head of databases at Tencent Cloud, led the Alibaba Cloud RDS kernel team, and helped open-source the AliSQL branch.
