The MariaDB Certified Database Administrator Associate exam is proctored and administered directly by MariaDB, letting DBAs demonstrate hands-on MariaDB expertise to employers and colleagues through an interactive, real-world-style test rather than multiple choice alone. It's offered both in person and remotely.

MariaDB recommends preparing with its three-day Standard DBA training course (online or on-site) or having at least a year of hands-on MariaDB experience before attempting it; a dedicated DBA Associate Certification Exam Prep course is also available to review the subject areas and exam format directly.
