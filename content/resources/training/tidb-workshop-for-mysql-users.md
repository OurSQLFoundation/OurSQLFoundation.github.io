PingCAP's "TiDB Workshop for MySQL Users" is a self-paced, hands-on course listed at the intermediate level and sized at around 5 hours of exercises. It's aimed at people already comfortable writing SQL and running DML/DDL against a MySQL-compatible database, who want to see where TiDB's SQL dialect, resource control, and placement rules diverge from MySQL.

The self-paced version runs through PingCAP's own education platform; the same workshop is also listed in PingCAP's training catalog as available via instructor-led Private Training for teams that want a custom delivery.
