"MySQL Security Hardening and Operational Automation" is a self-paced Pluralsight course taught by David Clinton, rated Intermediate and running about 53 minutes, last updated August 28, 2026. It teaches how to audit a MySQL configuration and maintain its security posture over the deployment's lifecycle, with exercises that assume a Linux/Bash environment (WSL or Git Bash on Windows).

The course is the fourth entry in Pluralsight's "Database Administration Fundamentals" learning path, where it's paired with a companion lab, "MySQL Performance & Security," alongside separate path courses on backup and recovery, high availability and failover, and performance monitoring and troubleshooting.

Access requires an active Pluralsight subscription.
