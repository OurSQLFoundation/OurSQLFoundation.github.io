"MySQL Essential Training" is a self-paced LinkedIn Learning course taught by Bill Weinman, released March 14, 2024, with a runtime of about 1 hour 58 minutes and an Intermediate skill rating. It covers the practical work of creating, updating, and maintaining MySQL databases. It replaces an earlier 2019 edition of the same title on the platform (formerly Lynda.com).

Access requires a LinkedIn Learning subscription, and completed courses can be added as a certificate to a LinkedIn profile.
