"MySQL Fundamentals" (Part 1) is a self-paced Pluralsight course taught by Pinal Dave, rated Beginner and running about 2 hours 37 minutes. It walks through installing MySQL and writing basic queries to retrieve and modify data, as a starting point for developers new to the database. It's part of a two-part series on Pluralsight; a follow-on "MySQL Fundamentals" (Part 2) course continues into more advanced querying.

Access requires an active Pluralsight subscription, and the course carries Pluralsight's usual completion tracking and skill assessments.
