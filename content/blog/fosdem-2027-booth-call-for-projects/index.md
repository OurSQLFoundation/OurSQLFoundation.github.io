FOSDEM is coming, and this year we want to bring some friends with us.

OurSQL Foundation is opening a call for open source MySQL-compatible projects to join us at FOSDEM 2027 in Brussels (January 30–31). If you build an open source project, alone or with a team, and you want more people to know about it, this is for you.

## What you get

**A place at our booth.** We will share our stand with you. You can run a demo, show what you built, or just talk with your users and future users. FOSDEM brings several thousand developers from Europe and all over the world, so it's a good crowd to meet.

**Up to $1,000 of support.** Use it for marketing materials, swag, or to cover part of your travel. You decide what helps you most.

## Who can apply

- Open source projects that are MySQL-compatible: tools, connectors, proxies, storage engines, extensions, utilities, anything that lives in the ecosystem
- Built by an individual or with a team
- Ready to come to Brussels and talk about your work

We can invite only five projects, so there will be a selection. Please don't be shy: small and young projects are very welcome.

## How to apply

Fill in the form by **November 30**: [Apply here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfwUQ424knUpwe_RlkxNegthznCgccpjpSnGYYppH0mpnwgNA/viewform)

We will contact every project that applies.

## One more thing: the devroom CFP is open

You can also give a talk to the biggest MySQL ecosystem audience of the year. **The M-Ecosystem devroom** at FOSDEM accepts proposals until **November 29**. We are co-organizers, and we support the whole ecosystem, so submit your talk, even if you think it's "too small" or "too niche."

Submit in the [FOSDEM call for proposals](https://pretalx.fosdem.org/fosdem-2027/cfp) and pick the track **"The M-ecosystem"**. More about the devroom in [our earlier post](/blog/m-ecosystem-devroom-fosdem-2027/).

Questions? Find us in [Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/oursqlfoundation/shared_invite/zt-480k6h62a-t0HalCPwvXUC_lpd4aNylA).

See you at FOSDEM!
